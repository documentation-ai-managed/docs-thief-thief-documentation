RTS Online offers robust streaming capabilities for both live broadcasts and on-demand content. Our streaming infrastructure supports high-quality video delivery with adaptive bitrate technology, ensuring optimal playback across all devices and network conditions.

<Callout kind="tip">For the best streaming experience, use a wired internet connection and close unnecessary applications.</Callout>

Our streaming platform uses advanced content delivery networks (CDNs) to ensure global accessibility and minimal latency. Whether you're in Belgrade or abroad, you can enjoy smooth, uninterrupted streaming of RTS content.

The on-demand library features thousands of hours of archived programming, including classic Serbian films, historical documentaries, and cultural programs. Our search and recommendation algorithms help you discover content that matches your interests.

For developers integrating streaming into their applications, we provide comprehensive APIs with detailed documentation. These APIs support features like playlist creation, content metadata retrieval, and custom player implementations.

Security is a top priority for our streaming services. All content is encrypted during transmission, and we implement digital rights management (DRM) for premium content to prevent unauthorized distribution.

Our adaptive streaming technology automatically adjusts video quality based on your network conditions, providing the best possible viewing experience without manual intervention. This ensures smooth playback even on variable connections.

RTS Online supports multiple audio tracks and subtitle options for many programs, making our content accessible to a wider audience. Users can choose from various languages and subtitle formats.

The platform includes parental controls and content ratings, allowing families to enjoy RTS programming safely. These features help filter content based on age appropriateness and thematic elements.

We continuously monitor streaming performance and user feedback to improve our services. Regular updates introduce new features like picture-in-picture mode, casting support, and enhanced accessibility options.

Whether you're watching breaking news coverage, enjoying a cultural documentary, or listening to live radio broadcasts, RTS Online's streaming setup ensures a seamless, high-quality media experience.
