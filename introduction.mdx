RTS Online is a leading online media platform providing the latest news and analysis from Serbia, the Balkans, and around the world. They offer live streaming of their radio and TV programs, as well as on-demand viewing of the best shows from Serbia's public media service.

<Callout kind="tip">For the best experience, ensure your device supports HTML5 video streaming and has a stable internet connection.</Callout>

To get started with RTS Online, simply visit our website and create a free account. You'll immediately gain access to breaking news, live radio streams, and a selection of on-demand TV shows. Our platform is designed to be intuitive, with powerful search capabilities and personalized recommendations based on your viewing history.

RTS Online integrates seamlessly with modern web technologies, making it easy to embed our content into your own applications or websites. Whether you're a developer looking to add news feeds to your app or a content creator wanting to include RTS streams in your media, our API provides comprehensive access to all our resources.

Our commitment to quality journalism and diverse programming sets us apart in the media landscape. From investigative reporting to cultural documentaries, RTS Online delivers content that informs, entertains, and connects communities across borders. With live streaming capabilities and a vast archive of historical broadcasts, we ensure that important moments in Serbian and Balkan history are preserved and accessible to all.

The platform supports multiple languages and provides subtitles for many programs, making our content accessible to a global audience. Our mobile applications offer the same rich experience as our web platform, ensuring you stay connected wherever you are.

As you explore RTS Online, you'll discover a treasure trove of media content that reflects the vibrant culture and current events of Serbia and the region. Whether you're interested in politics, sports, arts, or entertainment, our comprehensive coverage ensures you're always up-to-date with the latest developments.
